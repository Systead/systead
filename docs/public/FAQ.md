# Systead public FAQ

## What is Systead in plain English?

Systead is being built as one private place for complicated work that has outgrown scattered apps, folders, notes, chats, and memory.

It helps keep the work, its sources, its history, its unresolved questions, and its next actions connected.

## What is a House?

A **House** is your configured Systead environment. It is the place you return to for current work, knowledge, practical records, review, history, and any specialist products you choose to use.

It is not a public social profile and it is not a separate parent product.

## Is Systead available to download?

No. A private pre-alpha build is running, but there is no supported public tester package or installer yet.

## Is it only an idea or a website?

No. A private build exists and is being used under real daily workload. The website and repository explain the product and its boundaries; they are not the whole product.

## Why call it pre-alpha?

Because the build exists, but installation, migration, backup, restore, privacy, security, compatibility, update, removal, and support expectations have not yet been proven for outside users.

## Can I join the stress test?

Not currently. The first phase is private and intentionally focused on breaking assumptions safely inside the proving environment.

## What is AuthorMachine?

AuthorMachine by Systead is the first specialist product. It is designed to keep the moving parts around a book connected: manuscript state, canon, continuity, editions, production, metadata, catalogue, release work, publishing contacts, and reviewable assistance.

## Does AI run everything automatically?

No. AI may compare, summarize, classify, draft, detect conflicts, or prepare a proposed action. Important changes should remain visible and require suitable human authority.

Publishing, sending, payment, deletion, synchronization, and account changes are treated as explicit external effects.

## Does everything stay on the device?

Systead is local-first. Optional external integrations may still send data to a declared service when the user chooses and approves that operation.

The project does not currently make the absolute claim that nothing can ever leave the device.

## Is Systead secure?

Security is a design priority, but no production-grade security guarantee or completed independent audit is claimed during pre-alpha.

## Is Systead open source?

No blanket public software licence has been granted. Specific components may later have their own stated licences.

## Are the website images real screenshots?

They are public-safe pre-alpha interface studies based on the current product direction. They use synthetic data and are not presented as exact release screenshots.

## Who owns Systead today?

The current work was created before a Systead company exists, so its present legal owner is Marius Johan Stokknes. **Systead** is the product and brand name.

The intended future platform company is **Systead Systems S.L.U.** A future transfer or exclusive licence of the existing IP would need to be documented in writing after that company has been incorporated and registered.

## Does Stokknes Publishing own Systead?

No. The intended separation is:

- **Systead Systems S.L.U.** — future home of the Systead platform, software, brand, and technology IP.
- **Stokknes Publishing S.L.U.** — future publishing company for books, catalogues, editions, and publishing operations.

The publishing company may use AuthorMachine, but should not own the general platform merely because publishing was the first proving domain.

## When will Systead release?

No date is announced. Readiness depends on installation, migration, recovery, privacy, security, documentation, and support gates rather than a marketing deadline.
