# Systead public FAQ

## Is Systead available to download?

No. A private pre-alpha build is running, but there is no supported public tester package or installer.

## Is it only an idea or website?

No. A private build exists and is being used under real daily workload. The public site and repository describe the product and its boundaries; they are not the entire system.

## Why call it pre-alpha?

Because the build exists, but outside-user installation, migration, backup, restore, privacy, security, compatibility, update, and support expectations have not yet been proven.

## Can I join the stress test?

Not currently. The first stress-test phase is private and focused on breaking assumptions safely inside the proving environment.

## What is a House?

A House is one operator’s configured Systead environment: canonical records, knowledge, desks, modules, workflows, permissions, integrations, history, backup, and recovery state.

## Is “Systead House” a separate product?

No. “Systead House” may be used descriptively, but the standard environment term is **House**. Systead is the parent platform.

## What is Systead Core?

Core is the generic foundation for records, storage, knowledge, proposals, commands, review, audit, boundaries, integrations, export, backup, and recovery.

## What is AuthorMachine?

AuthorMachine by Systead is the first specialist product. It focuses on manuscripts, projects, canon, continuity, editions, production, catalogue, rights, launch operations, publishing contacts, knowledge, and reviewable assistance.

## Is Stokknes House the public demo?

No. Stokknes House is the private flagship and proving ground. Public examples use synthetic or deliberately sanitized data.

## Does AI run everything automatically?

No. AI may prepare analysis, drafts, comparisons, proposals, or commands. Material changes require appropriate human approval. Publishing, sending, payment, deletion, synchronization, and account changes are explicit external effects.

## Is all data guaranteed to stay on-device?

Core is local-first, but optional external integrations may send data to declared services. Any future public feature must state what leaves the House and why. The project does not currently make an absolute “nothing ever leaves the device” claim.

## Is Systead secure?

Security is a design priority, but no production-grade security guarantee or completed independent audit is currently claimed.

## Is Systead open source?

No blanket public software licence has been granted. A specific component may later state its own licence.

## When will it release?

No date is announced. Readiness is based on installation, migration, recovery, privacy, security, documentation, and support gates rather than a marketing deadline.

## Are the website images real screenshots?

They are public-safe pre-alpha interface studies based on the current architecture and private-build direction. They use synthetic data and are not presented as exact release screenshots.
