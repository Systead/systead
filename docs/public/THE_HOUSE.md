
# The House

A **House** is one operator's configured Systead environment.

It is the lived product: the place the operator returns to, works inside, searches, reviews, and gradually trusts.

A House may contain:

- Today and active-work surfaces;
- selected Rooms and Desks;
- projects, records, knowledge, decisions, and practical responsibilities;
- people and relevant commitments;
- House Steward assistance;
- optional specialist Programs;
- declared Connectors;
- backups, recovery, audit, and Exit Kit tools.

## Progressive disclosure

The product should have three depths:

1. **Calm starting surface** — what changed, what matters, quick capture, search, and active areas.
2. **Working Rooms** — records, relationships, decisions, status, next actions, and domain tools.
3. **Machinery underneath** — provenance, canonical standing, conflicts, authority, commands, audit, recovery, schemas, and system health.

The private flagship is deliberately dense because it carries accumulated real-world complexity. Public Systead should preserve that depth without requiring a new operator to face it all at once.

## Durability rule

> **Programs are replaceable. The House is durable.**

Removing a Program should not destroy unrelated House memory. Changing tools should not erase why decisions were made. Export should preserve important meaning in human-readable and machine-readable forms.
