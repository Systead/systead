A manageable commission

One person.
One active build.
A clear handover.

Direct work with the person building your site. A defined scope protects both the quality of the result and the time it takes.

  1. Define the useful version

    Agree purpose, pages, content, revision rounds and acceptance criteria. Use the estimator to prepare a brief.

  2. Design, build and check

    Responsive layouts, metadata and technical SEO foundations, plus an accessibility and performance pass.

  3. Launch on your accounts

    One agreed deployment, domain connection and a practical handover. You own and pay for your domain and hosting directly.

  4. Choose follow-up deliberately

    Optional care comes in fixed blocks. No required subscription and no unlimited support promise.
