Why it exists

The problem is not that we lack tools. It is that none of them know the whole situation.

One app knows the task. Another has the file. A chat contains the decision. A spreadsheet has the number. Your own memory is expected to hold the connection between all of them.

That arrangement works until the work becomes long-running, personal, messy, or important. Then every new tool adds another place to check, another copy that can drift, and another quiet dependency on remembering what happened three months ago.

Systead starts from a different assumption: the work should be able to remember itself. The source, the decision, the current state, the unresolved question, and the next action should remain connected.

It is not trying to replace judgment. It is trying to stop judgment from being wasted on finding the same context again and again.

Inside a House

One place to return to. Different rooms for different kinds of work.

A House is your configured Systead environment. It can stay small, or grow into a serious working system without forcing every kind of information into the same generic screen.

01Today

See what changed and what deserves attention.

A calm starting point for active work, review requests, unresolved risks, recent changes, and the decisions that cannot be automated away.

02Work

Keep projects, obligations, files, and decisions connected.

The point is not another task list. It is knowing why something exists, what it depends on, who or what it affects, and what “done” actually means.

03Knowledge

Remember the source, not only the summary.

Notes, manuals, research, evidence, history, and previous choices remain tied to the records and work they explain.

04Practical life

Give recurring reality somewhere sensible to live.

Contacts, assets, services, household responsibilities, recurring maintenance, and other practical records can be managed without turning the product into a lifestyle feed.

05Specialist rooms

Add depth only where the work needs it.

Purpose-built products can sit inside the same House and share its memory, review, privacy, and recovery foundations. AuthorMachine is the first.

First specialist product

AuthorMachine by Systead

A book stops being “one document” almost immediately.

There are drafts, edits, canon decisions, characters, research, covers, formats, metadata, prices, proofs, release plans, reviewers, storefronts, corrections, and the uncomfortable question of which file is actually current.

AuthorMachine is being built to hold that entire publishing reality together while leaving the author in control of the work.

Project

Know the actual state of the book.

Current draft, open questions, production stage, next judgment, risks, and readiness—not a fake percentage pretending to know when art is finished.

Canon

Keep the world from quietly contradicting itself.

Characters, locations, timelines, rules, style decisions, contradictions, and unresolved continuity questions remain linked to evidence.

Editions

Stop treating every exported file as the master.

Manuscript, proof, paperback, hardcover, ebook, translation, metadata, pricing, and release state can be tracked separately.

Release

Run publishing as work, not panic.

Checklists, contacts, ARC activity, catalogue changes, approved exports, external destinations, and post-release corrections stay visible.

Under the floorboards

The heavier system work lives here.

The simple product experience depends on strict foundations underneath. This is where Systead becomes more technical: records, commands, boundaries, recovery, modules, and integration contracts.

Systead Core

The shared foundation for records, storage, knowledge, review, commands, audit, export, backup, recovery, and declared integrations.

Canonical records

Modules link stable entities instead of creating drifting copies of people, projects, files, publications, assets, decisions, and obligations.

Controlled commands

Material actions declare intent, source, scope, affected records, risk, approval requirement, verification, and recovery path.

Privacy boundaries

Access does not equal permission to publish, sync, email, export, index externally, or send private material to an outside model.

Specialist modules

Domain products can add deep workflows while sharing the same safety, memory, and portability contracts.

Exit and recovery

A House should remain inspectable, exportable, restorable, and usable without being trapped inside one cloud account or vendor promise.

Material action lifecycle

Prepare first. Act second. Leave evidence.

Risk changes how much preview, approval, verification, and recovery a command requires.

  1. ProposeState the intended change and why.
  2. PreviewShow scope, effects, and affected records.
  3. ApproveBind authority to the exact proposal.
  4. ExecutePerform only the approved operation.
  5. VerifyCheck the expected result.
  6. RecoverRollback, restore, or correct safely.
