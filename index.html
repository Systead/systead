The alpha licence

Buy the build.
Keep the build.

The commercial model under test is deliberately simple: one payment for the current major build. No monthly rent. No consultancy required. A purchased House remains usable and exportable on its own.

01No subscription required

The alpha is a product licence, not a meter running in the background.

02Future versions are optional

A later major version must earn another purchase. It cannot disable the one already owned.

03Exit is part of the product

Readable export and recovery are working requirements, not a cancellation-page promise.

Build what the work needs. Prove it under real pressure. Sell the result only when another House can leave with itself intact.
