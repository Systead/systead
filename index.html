What Systead is

Not another dashboard. An owned operating environment.

A dashboard shows information. A House connects the records, rules, evidence, commands, review states, and recovery paths required to run ongoing work without creating five conflicting versions of the truth.

01

Canonical records

People, projects, assets, publications, decisions, obligations, and other entities are linked through stable records rather than copied into isolated modules.

02

Operational knowledge

Manuals, source files, evidence, history, style rules, and decisions remain searchable and connected to the work they explain.

03

Reviewable commands

Material actions declare their source, intended effect, scope, risk, confirmation requirement, verification step, and recovery route.

04

Specialist systems

Domain products use the same Core contracts while adding purpose-built records and workflows. AuthorMachine is the first.

Canonical product model

One parent platform. One configured House. Specialist systems where they belong.

The names are deliberately separated so the platform does not become trapped inside its first use case or confused with one private environment.

Systead
The parent brand, platform, and universal operating architecture.
Systead Core
Generic records, storage, knowledge, commands, review, safety, audit, integration contracts, export, backup, and recovery.
House
One operator’s configured environment: terminology, selected desks, modules, workflows, permissions, data, and optional integrations.
AuthorMachine
The first specialist product, focused on publishing operations and built on shared Systead foundations.
Stokknes House
The private flagship and proving ground. Reusable architecture may be extracted; its personal records and configuration are never distributed as demo data.
Controlled execution

AI may prepare the work. Authority remains explicit.

The system is designed around declared effects rather than vague automation. Higher-risk actions require stronger evidence, clearer previews, narrower authority, and a usable recovery path.

Propose

State the intended change, source, reason, assumptions, and confidence.

Preview

Show affected records, files, destinations, integrations, and likely consequences.

Approve

Obtain the required human authority for the declared risk and exact scope.

Execute

Perform only the operation that was previewed and approved.

Verify

Check the expected result and report mismatches or partial failure.

Audit

Record what changed, when, why, from which source, and under whose authority.

Recover

Rollback, restore, quarantine, or perform a documented corrective action.

First specialist product

AuthorMachine by Systead

AuthorMachine is where the architecture is being forced to survive a real, difficult domain: long-running creative projects, many files and editions, contradictory notes, release operations, external publishing systems, and decisions that must remain traceable.

Book and project cockpits

Canonical project state, next decisions, risks, open issues, evidence, and production readiness in one operational view.

Canon and continuity

Character, timeline, location, style, decision, contradiction, and plot-hole records linked to their source material.

Edition and production control

Separate manuscript, proof, edition, format, metadata, catalogue, rights, pricing, and release states instead of treating every exported file as truth.

Publishing operations

Launch rooms, ARC and reviewer workflows, publishing contacts, release checklists, approved exports, and external destinations.

Reviewable assistance

AI can compare, summarize, classify, draft, detect conflicts, and prepare changes—but does not silently overwrite canon or publish work.

Generalization pressure

Capabilities useful outside publishing are extracted into Core. Publishing assumptions remain inside AuthorMachine.

Current development status

The build exists. The product promise is still being earned.

Pre-alpha is the correct label because the system is running privately, but its architecture, migration behavior, error handling, installation path, security posture, and public boundaries have not yet been proven for outside users.

AreaCurrent honest status
Running systemA private build is operating in the flagship proving environment and is being used under real daily workload.

Data and privacyPublic material uses synthetic or deliberately sanitized examples. Private House data is not public demo content.
SecurityNo claim of production-grade security, formal audit, hardened multi-user isolation, or complete threat-model coverage.
LicensingNo blanket public software licence is granted unless a specific component explicitly states otherwise.
Release dateNot announced. Readiness gates matter more than inventing a date before recovery, portability, and boundaries have been proven.

What the first stress-test phase is trying to break

Conflicting records, duplicates, stale references, and modules that clone truth instead of linking it.
Commands that cannot clearly explain their scope, evidence, side effects, verification, or recovery route.
Imports and indexes that confuse private source material with public output or reusable fixtures.
Publishing-specific assumptions leaking into Core and private Stokknes configuration leaking into product defaults.
Failures that leave the operator unsure what changed, what did not change, and what the next safe action is.
