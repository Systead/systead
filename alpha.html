What “controlled” means

The operator sees the change before Systead makes it.

Fast does not mean frantic. The alpha earns trust by making intent, affected records, risk, approval, verification, and recovery visible in one place.

The contractWhat a purchase must mean
01

The purchased build remains usable.

A future release cannot reach backward and disable the House already owned.

02

Future major versions are optional purchases.

Another version must earn another payment through clear new value. Staying on the owned build remains a valid choice.

03

Readable export, documented records, and recovery must work without requiring a support contract or continued payment.

Plain answers

Before anybody buys anything.

Is Systead becoming a subscription?

The Founding Alpha is explicitly being designed as a one-time product licence. Hosted or synchronization services, if they ever exist, would need their own separate justification.

Does the alpha require consultancy?

No. A bounded import path and clear documentation are product requirements. Personal implementation services are not the route being pursued.

Is AuthorMachine the release name?

No. AuthorMachine remains the internal name for the private publishing proving line. The beta-facing Program remains Publishing House until a better cleared name earns adoption.

Is Shared Home already a released product?

No. It is the second active proving direction and exists in the Alpha 00 synthetic House. Its purpose is to prove profiles, shared responsibility, tenancy evidence, and clean exit.

Can the operator leave?

They must be able to. A House without understandable export, recovery, and removal behaviour is not ready to be sold as Systead.

The Founding Alpha contract is directional until release terms, price, platform support, and enabled scope are formally published.
