What “controlled” means

The operator sees the change before Systead makes it.

Fast does not mean frantic. The private pre-alpha earns trust by making intent, affected records, risk, approval, verification, and recovery visible in one place.

Plain answers

Before anybody buys anything.

Can I download or use Systead now?

No. The current system is a private pre-alpha under real internal use. There is no public tester package or supported installer yet.

Is Systead becoming a subscription?

The intended commercial direction is a one-time product licence. Hosted or synchronization services, if they ever exist, would need their own separate justification.

Does the alpha require consultancy?

No. A bounded import path and clear documentation are product requirements. Personal implementation services are not the route being pursued.

Is AuthorMachine the release name?

No. AuthorMachine remains the internal name for the private publishing proving line. Folio is the pre-alpha codename; Publishing House is the descriptive working label. None is a cleared release name.

What is intended to be free?

The Systead application and a useful local House foundation: capture, generic records, search, decisions, review, backup, recovery, and readable exit. Specialist Programs provide the paid domain depth. Final public licence terms remain open.

Is Shared Home already a released product?

No. It is the second active proving direction and exists in the private pre-alpha 00 synthetic House. Its purpose is to prove profiles, shared responsibility, tenancy evidence, and clean exit.

Is Property Steward separate from Shared Home?

Yes. Hearth is resident-side household continuity; Keystone is owner-side property continuity. They may exchange an explicitly shared maintenance or handover record, but not silently share private House data or authority.

Can the operator leave?

They must be able to. A House without understandable export, recovery, and removal behaviour is not ready to be sold as Systead.

Systead is a private pre-alpha and is not available for public use. Licence direction, price, platform support, Program names, and enabled scope remain provisional.
