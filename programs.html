Shared Home

A household should not run on whoever remembers everything.

A home carries people, money, agreements, services, repairs, documents, possessions, access, recurring obligations, and evidence. Co-renters and renters also need a fair account of what happened and who is waiting on whom.

What it helps with Ordinary moments that become stressful when the history is missing.
Co-rent

Shared costs without shared confusion.

Record the agreed split, what was paid, the receipt, and what remains—without becoming a payment processor.

Repairs

One evidence trail from fault to fix.

Keep photographs, dates, messages, appointments, costs, responsibility, and resolution together.

Moving

A clean handover instead of archaeology.

Carry condition, keys, deposits, ownership, services, agreements, and open issues into move-out or the next resident.

Daily home

Responsibility that does not live in one head.

See recurring upkeep, warranties, providers, important documents, shared items, and who owns the next step.

The Program contract

Specialist vocabulary. Shared truth.

RecordsPrograms add domain records. Core retains shared people, files, decisions, obligations, assets, and history.
ProfilesA profile changes the starting configuration—not the underlying truth or exit format.
AuthorityA Program may propose and stage. It cannot bypass permissions, review, audit, or recovery.
RemovalUninstalling a Program cannot silently erase the House or make shared records unreadable.
ExitEvery Program declares how its records, files, and history leave with the operator.
