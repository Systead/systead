The Program contract

Specialist vocabulary. Shared truth.

RecordsPrograms add domain records. Core retains shared people, files, decisions, obligations, assets, and history.
ProfilesA profile changes the starting configuration—not the underlying truth or exit format.
AuthorityA Program may propose and stage. It cannot bypass permissions, review, audit, or recovery.
RemovalUninstalling a Program cannot silently erase the House or make shared records unreadable.
ExitEvery Program declares how its records, files, and history leave with the operator.
