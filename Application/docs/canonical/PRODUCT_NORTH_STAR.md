# Product north star

**Status:** Canonical direction
**Maturity:** Pre-alpha
**Effective:** 19 July 2026

## The target

Systead is intended to become:

> **The safest and most coherent private system for long-lived human work.**

This is a broader target than building a better notes application, task manager, writing tool, database, or AI assistant.

Systead should preserve continuity between:

- memory;
- evidence;
- decisions;
- responsibility;
- action;
- history;
- recovery.

## The category

The working category is **private continuity system**.

Alternative explanatory language may include:

- local-first continuity and operations platform;
- private institutional memory for an individual or small owner-operated organisation;
- operator-owned environment for memory, decisions, and controlled action;
- durable digital estate.

These descriptions are explanatory. The public product name remains Systead.

## The central problem

Important work is fragmented across applications that each understand only one part of the situation.

The user is forced to remember:

- which record is current;
- why it became current;
- where the supporting evidence lives;
- which commitments depend on it;
- what changed since the last session;
- what will be affected by the next action;
- how to recover when an operation fails.

Systead should reduce that reconstruction burden.

## The three promises

### Memory

The House remembers current standing, sources, history, uncertainty, conflicts, and dependencies—not only text.

### Safety

Material actions expose intent, scope, effects, authority, verification, and recovery before they become invisible external consequences.

### Control

The operator owns the House, remains the decision authority, and can inspect, export, restore, or leave the system.

## The calm-surface rule

Internal ambition may be large. The visible product must remain calm.

The release discipline is:

> **Five useful surfaces first. Depth when requested. Machinery when necessary.**

A public House should not expose the full complexity of the private proving ground on first launch.

## Signature experiences

Systead should be recognizable through a small set of interactions:

- **Bring me back** — reconstruct a project after absence.
- **Why is this true?** — expose evidence, standing, and contradiction.
- **What changes if I do this?** — show consequences before action.
- **Resolve this safely** — prepare, approve, execute, verify, and recover.
- **What needs me?** — surface human judgment rather than every available task.

## Program strategy

Systead is built as a broader platform from the beginning.

The first specialist Program is publishing. Its current internal working name is **AuthorMachine**.

The commercial and release strategy may use the publishing Program as the first focused reason to adopt Systead, but the architecture and public platform story must not reduce Systead to an author application.

## Success test

Systead is moving toward the north star when it can reliably answer:

1. What is current?
2. Why is it current?
3. What changed?
4. What remains uncertain?
5. What depends on this?
6. What requires judgment?
7. What will the proposed action affect?
8. How will the result be verified?
9. How can the House recover?
10. Can the operator leave with understandable records?

## Failure modes to resist

- a beautiful mansion with too many rooms and no clear entrance;
- a generic productivity suite with a new vocabulary;
- an AI agent that hides authority behind convenience;
- a publishing product accidentally presented as the whole platform;
- a private personal build copied into public defaults;
- a local-first claim that still depends on opaque hosted authority;
- feature growth without migration, recovery, or exit discipline;
- technical documentation replacing an understandable product story.
