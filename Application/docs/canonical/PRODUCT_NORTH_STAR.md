
# Product north star

## One-sentence definition

> **Systead builds private continuity infrastructure for people and small organisations whose work is too important to remain scattered across tools and memory.**

## Human promise

> **Your work should not forget itself.**

## Product distinction

> **Programs solve particular domains. Systead preserves the whole.**

Systead is the platform and broader product direction. A House is the operator's configured environment. Programs are optional specialist applications inside that House.

## Root problem

Important work is fragmented across software that does not share memory, authority, provenance, or history. The operator becomes the integration layer.

Systead should preserve continuity between:

- current standing;
- source and evidence;
- decisions and reasoning;
- responsibility and dependency;
- uncertainty and contradiction;
- proposed action and consequence;
- verification and recovery;
- specialist Programs and the wider House.

## Core promises

### Memory

The House retains more than text. It preserves standing, source, history, decisions, uncertainty, contradiction, and dependency.

### Safety

Material actions expose scope, affected records, destinations, risk, authority, verification, and recovery.

### Control

The operator remains the authority. The House remains inspectable, interruptible, portable, and recoverable.

## Signature interactions

1. **Bring me back.** Reconstruct where I was, what changed, what remains unresolved, and what I should read first.
2. **Why is this true?** Show source, decision history, uncertainty, contradiction, and verification.
3. **What changes if I do this?** Show affected records, commitments, Programs, destinations, and automations before approval.
4. **Resolve this safely.** Preserve state, stage the correction, execute only what was approved, verify, and retain recovery.
5. **What needs me?** Surface blocked responsibilities, expiring commitments, conflicts, and judgments that cannot be automated away.

## Intended audience

People and small organisations carrying long-lived context and consequence, including independent professionals, creators, researchers, founders, complex households, and owner-operated organisations.

Do not market Systead as “for everyone.” Explain the problem clearly enough that appropriate users recognise themselves.

## Long-term category

> **Private institutional memory and controlled operations for people who are not institutions.**

This is a direction, not a claim of current maturity or a feature promise.

## Product discipline

The internal ambition may be broad. The visible product must remain calm.

> **Five useful surfaces first. Depth when requested. Machinery when necessary.**

## Current proof

The private flagship House and Program 01 publishing line demonstrate that a working system can exist under real daily complexity.

They do not prove public installation, generalisation, security, migration, onboarding, support, or release readiness.
