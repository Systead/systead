# Brand and naming

**Status:** Canonical working guidance
**Effective:** 2026-07-19

## Locked working names

- **Systead** — parent brand and platform.
- **Systead Core** — generic platform foundation.
- **House** — configured operator environment.
- **AuthorMachine by Systead** — first specialist product.
- **Stokknes House** — private flagship and proving ground.

Systead.com is the active primary domain asset for the platform.

## Naming rules

1. Write **Systead**, not “SysTead”, “Sys Stead”, or “Systead OS” as the default public name.
2. Write **AuthorMachine** as one word and use **AuthorMachine by Systead** on first public reference.
3. Use **House** for the configured environment concept.
4. Do not imply that Stokknes House is the product every customer receives.
5. Do not use AuthorMachine as the name of generic Systead infrastructure.
6. Use descriptive module names that survive expansion beyond one user or one industry.
7. Avoid claims such as “fully secure”, “autonomous”, “production-ready”, or “complete” before they are verifiably true.

## Public positioning

Primary line:

> Local-first command systems. Operator-owned, explainable, and reversible.

Expanded description:

> Systead is a local-first command platform for people who need one place to run work, knowledge, money, household operations, and specialist systems without surrendering control of their data or decisions.

## Brand character

Systead should feel:

- controlled rather than sterile;
- serious rather than corporate-generic;
- durable rather than trendy;
- technically credible without pretending certainty;
- private by design without sounding paranoid;
- powerful without selling autonomous replacement of human judgment.

## Visual status

Current marks are candidates, not legally or visually locked. Before final selection, review:

- originality and similarity risk;
- trademark and design clearance;
- small-size and favicon legibility;
- monochrome use;
- app-icon use;
- light and dark backgrounds;
- relation to, but distinction from, the Stokknes Publishing House identity.
