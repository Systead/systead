# Working names and release identity

**Status:** Canonical naming policy
**Effective:** 19 July 2026

## Locked parent brand

**Systead** is the active parent platform and brand.

It may be used publicly now.

## House

**House** is the standard term for an operator's configured Systead environment.

“Systead House” may be used descriptively when context requires it, but it is not a competing parent product.

## AuthorMachine

**AuthorMachine** is an internal working name for the first specialist publishing Program and the current private v10 line.

It is not yet locked as the public release name.

Public material must state, where relevant, that:

- the final release name may change;
- packaging may change;
- feature boundaries may change;
- individual capabilities may move into Core, remain experimental, ship later, or be removed;
- private v10 screenshots do not create a release promise.

## Program 01

**Program 01: Publishing** is the stable architectural label used when the product name is undecided.

## House Steward

**House Steward** is the current working name for the intelligence and assistance layer. The name may change before release.

## Stokknes House

**Stokknes House** is the private flagship and proving ground.

It is not a product name, public template, or demo dataset.

## Systead Systems S.L.U.

**Systead Systems S.L.U.** is an intended future company name and ownership destination, subject to legal formation and clearance.

It must not be presented as an existing legal entity before incorporation and registration.

## Stokknes Publishing

Stokknes Publishing is the intended publishing-company lane.

It should not be presented as the owner of Systead Core or the general platform merely because Program 01 serves publishing first.

## Naming-change procedure

A working name becomes a release name only after a recorded decision covers:

- search and conflict review;
- domain and handle position;
- trademark/legal review appropriate to the stage;
- product scope;
- relationship to Systead;
- migration of repository and documentation references;
- customer-facing transition plan;
- ownership record.
