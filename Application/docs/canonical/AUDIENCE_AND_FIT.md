
# Audience and fit

## Intended audience

Systead is intended for individuals, households, independent professionals, creators, founders, researchers, and small owner-operated organisations carrying long-lived context and consequence.

Early high-fit groups may include:

- independent professionals;
- authors and publishers;
- researchers and consultants;
- creators with complex catalogues;
- founders and owner-operators;
- households with substantial administration;
- small organisations requiring continuity without enterprise infrastructure.

## Recognition statements

A likely user may say:

- my work no longer fits inside one app;
- I keep rebuilding context;
- I need systems without project-management theatre;
- I want AI assistance without surrendering authority;
- I need to know what is current and why;
- I want custom-system power without maintaining a graveyard of scripts;
- I care what happens to these records years from now.

## Explicit non-fit at current maturity

Systead is not currently aimed at:

- casual note-taking;
- large real-time collaborative teams;
- enterprise ERP replacement;
- social workspaces;
- autonomous control over sensitive operations;
- users requiring a mature supported hosted service;
- users requiring a supported public release today.

## Audience rule

Do not market Systead as “for everyone.” Explain the problem clearly enough that appropriate users recognise themselves.
