# Future Program probes

**Status:** Approved directions for architecture testing
**Release standing:** Unscheduled; not available for public use

These probes test whether Systead's House and Program boundaries generalize beyond Publishing and Shared Home. Approval as a probe does not create a release promise, public name, delivery date, or feature commitment.

## Probe 03 — Property Steward

**Internal codename:** Keystone
**Primary side:** owner, owner-occupier, or small rental operator

Possible profiles:

- Owner-occupier;
- Single-property landlord;
- Small rental operator;
- Heritage-property steward.

Possible depth:

- property and tenancy standing;
- documents, inspections, obligations, and renewals;
- maintenance, vendors, visits, costs, and evidence;
- inventory, condition, keys, and access;
- communication and handover history.

Property Steward may reuse bounded modules with Shared Home. It may not silently expose resident records, reinterpret resident evidence, provide legal advice, collect rent, or exercise landlord authority.

## Probe 04 — Archive & Legacy

**Internal codename:** Chronicle

Possible profiles:

- Family Archive;
- Historian;
- Collector;
- Local Heritage;
- Estate and Legacy.

Possible depth:

- sources, citations, claims, and confidence;
- people, places, organisations, and relationships;
- timelines and event reconstruction;
- photographs, letters, recordings, objects, and provenance;
- oral histories and interview permissions;
- collection condition, preservation, and digitization;
- inheritance, handover, and readable legacy export.

Chronicle is especially valuable as a test of long-lived context, conflicting evidence, provenance, preservation, and portability.

## Probe 05 — Animal Care

**Internal codename:** Sanctuary

Possible profiles:

- Multi-animal household;
- Independent caretaker;
- Foster network;
- Refuge or shelter;
- Veterinary-support handover.

Possible depth:

- animal identity, origin, documents, and relationships;
- daily routines, feeding, behaviour, and care plans;
- appointment, treatment, medication, and vaccination history;
- foster, transfer, adoption, and handover;
- supplies, equipment, facilities, and responsibilities;
- evidence and communication across carers and professionals.

Sanctuary does not diagnose, prescribe, replace veterinary records, or claim clinical authority. Veterinary-practice software would require a separate regulated scope and should not be implied by this probe.

## Wild architecture probes

These remain lower-priority questions rather than approved Programs:

- **Sourcehouse:** historian, genealogist, or investigative research with claims, citations, conflicts, and timelines;
- **Collection Steward:** private collections, small museums, provenance, condition, conservation, and exhibition history;
- **Restoration Workshop:** objects, heritage buildings, materials, interventions, photographs, craftspeople, and reversible decisions;
- **Field Station:** expeditions, ecological monitoring, samples, permits, equipment, observations, and handovers;
- **Community House:** clubs, cooperatives, associations, decisions, members, shared assets, and institutional memory;
- **Creative Production:** film, music, games, and other multi-output creative projects adjacent to Publishing.

No probe should enter implementation merely because it is interesting. It should earn priority through repeated pain, reachable operators, strong Core reuse, bounded authority, low integration burden, and a credible one-time purchase.
