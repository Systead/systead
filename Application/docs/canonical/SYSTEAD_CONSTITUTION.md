# Systead constitution

**Status:** Canonical product law
**Applies to:** Core, Houses, Rooms, Desks, Programs, modules, connectors, automation, AI assistance, imports, exports, and release tooling

These rules are not marketing language. They are intended to constrain implementation and product decisions.

## 1. The operator owns the House

The operator must be able to inspect, move, export, back up, restore, archive, and leave with their records.

A remote service may extend the House. It must not quietly become its true owner.

## 2. No destructive action happens silently

Deletion, irreversible transformation, identity merge, rights change, publication, payment, or other material external effect requires risk-appropriate preview, authority, and evidence.

## 3. Important conclusions expose their evidence

The system should distinguish source, inference, estimate, interpretation, imported claim, and unresolved conflict.

A confident sentence is not evidence.

## 4. Uncertainty is represented rather than hidden

Unknown, disputed, stale, incomplete, or unverified information must not be converted into clean certainty merely to simplify the interface.

## 5. Canonical records can be challenged and corrected

Canonical standing is not permanent dogma. The House must support review, supersession, correction, conflict reporting, and historical reconstruction.

## 6. Programs cannot bypass Core safety

A specialist Program, connector, or plugin may add domain depth. It may not invent a private authority system that bypasses shared permission, review, audit, privacy, export, or recovery contracts.

## 7. Private information does not cross boundaries accidentally

Access to a record does not equal permission to publish, synchronize, email, export, analyze remotely, or include it in an outside model prompt.

## 8. Automation remains inspectable and interruptible

Automation should declare trigger, scope, rule, proposed effect, authority, logs, verification, and failure state.

The operator must be able to pause, review, or revoke it.

## 9. The House remains exportable

Portability and exit are part of trust, not post-release polish.

Exports should be documented, inspectable, and testable by restore or independent reading.

## 10. AI assists judgment; it does not impersonate certainty

AI may observe, summarize, compare, detect, recommend, draft, and prepare.

It must not silently represent operator approval, overwrite canonical truth, or hide uncertainty behind confident language.

## 11. Recovery is part of the action

Important operations must have a declared rollback, restore, quarantine, correction, or compensating-action path appropriate to their risk.

## 12. Public examples do not consume private lives

Private House records are not demo content merely because they exist. Public screenshots, fixtures, manuals, and tests should use synthetic or deliberately approved material.

## 13. The system tells the truth about its maturity

A running private build is not a concept. It is also not a supported alpha, beta, or production product until installation, migration, security, recovery, documentation, and support gates are met.

## 14. The next safe decision matters more than the largest feature count

The interface should help the operator understand what matters, what changed, what is uncertain, what is blocked, what authority applies, and what the next safe action is.

## 15. The product serves the person; the person does not serve the system

Systead should reduce reconstruction, maintenance, and invisible coordination work. It must not create a new obligation to constantly curate the House for the House's own sake.
