
# Problems and use cases

## Root problem

Important work is fragmented across tools that do not share memory, authority, provenance, or history. The operator becomes the integration layer.

## Core problem families

### Version and standing uncertainty

The record exists, but its current standing is unclear. The House must distinguish current, working, historical, imported, derived, disputed, superseded, quarantined, and archived state.

### Decision amnesia

The outcome remains while reasoning, evidence, alternatives, constraints, and expected consequences disappear.

### Context collapse after interruption

The operator returns after illness, overload, travel, changed employment, or ordinary delay and must reconstruct the situation from fragments.

### Unsafe intelligence and automation

AI or automation can generate, classify, move, publish, or change material faster than the operator can understand or reverse.

### Tool accumulation

Each new application, script, or service creates another data model, boundary, migration burden, and source of contradiction.

### Fragile digital estate

Years of work depend on one device, one service, one undocumented script, one developer, or one person's memory.

### Domain silos

Specialist tools understand one domain but cannot preserve continuity with the rest of the operator's House.

## Signature use cases

- Bring me back.
- Why is this true?
- What changes if I do this?
- Resolve this safely.
- What needs me?

These interactions should remain more important than raw feature count.
