
# Differentiation

Systead should not be positioned merely as a replacement for a note app, task manager, database builder, writing tool, AI assistant, or custom script collection.

Those categories may provide useful surfaces or inputs. Systead's intended differentiation is the combination of:

- canonical memory;
- decision continuity;
- provenance and uncertainty;
- consequence-aware controlled action;
- cross-Program continuity;
- time-aware reconstruction;
- operator authority;
- recovery and exit.

## Category distinction

Other tools may help create, organise, schedule, search, or generate information.

Systead should help the work remain coherent over time.

## Differentiation rule

Do not claim superiority through vague feature comparisons or invented performance numbers. Demonstrate differentiation through signature interactions, architecture, real recovery evidence, clear authority boundaries, and successful exit tests.
