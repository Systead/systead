# Program 03: Property Steward

**Public pre-alpha label:** Property Steward
**Internal codename:** Keystone
**Final release name:** Undecided
**Maturity:** Third focus Program and synthetic implementation target

## Purpose

Program 03 gives owner-occupiers, small landlords, and property stewards a durable, understandable record of how a property is operated.

It is owner-side continuity: what exists, who occupies or serves it, which condition and tenancy standing is current, what needs attention, what evidence supports a decision, and what must survive a handover.

## Founding pre-alpha profiles

- **Owner-Occupier** for a person maintaining the building and ownership side of their own home;
- **Single-Property Landlord** for one rented property with a small number of tenancies;
- **Small Rental Operator** for a bounded portfolio without enterprise property-management complexity;
- **Property Steward** for a person preserving a family, heritage, or mixed-use property over time.

Profiles configure one Program. They may change starting desks, language, and views, but must not fork canonical record types or weaken authority rules.

## Founding pre-alpha desks

- property, unit, space, and responsibility standing;
- tenancy, resident, deposit, handover, and communication history;
- condition, inspection, photographic evidence, keys, and access;
- maintenance issue, triage, quote, work, cost, and verified resolution;
- vendor, provider, contract, and work history;
- documents, insurance, certificates, obligations, and renewals.

## Resident and owner boundary

Property Steward is not a resident-surveillance or automated landlord-enforcement product.

- A property owner does not gain access to a resident's private Shared Home records.
- A resident record and owner record may refer to the same issue only through an explicit shared link or handover package.
- Visibility, source, standing, and changes remain reviewable on both sides.
- A claim made by one side does not silently become canonical truth for the other.
- External messages, payments, notices, filings, or access actions require explicit authority, Connectors, and review.

## Initial exclusions

The founding pre-alpha does not claim:

- rent collection or payment processing;
- legal, tenancy, tax, safety, or compliance advice;
- jurisdiction-specific notices or filings;
- enterprise portfolio, facilities, or workforce management;
- covert monitoring, smart-lock control, or building automation;
- autonomous contractor booking, resident communication, or enforcement.

## Relationship to Core

Generic people, organisations, places, records, files, assets, obligations, decisions, tasks, evidence, review, search, export, and recovery remain House/Core capabilities.

Property Steward owns the owner-side schema and workflows that give those shared capabilities property meaning. Removing the Program must not make unrelated House memory unreadable or trap the operator's data.

## Relationship to Shared Home

Program 02 and Program 03 may reuse bounded contracts:

- Property Record;
- Maintenance & Evidence;
- Inventory & Condition;
- Important Documents;
- Tenancy & Handover.

Reuse does not imply shared authority. Someone who lives in one property and stewards another may install both Programs in one House, while their resident-side and owner-side records remain deliberately separated.

## Founding pre-alpha evidence

Pre-alpha 03 is successful when a synthetic owner-occupier, single-property landlord, and small rental-operator House can:

1. create and find current property, unit, tenancy, and condition standing;
2. preserve a maintenance thread from report through verified resolution;
3. link evidence, quotes, vendors, documents, costs, and decisions without hidden external effects;
4. exchange a bounded maintenance or handover record with Shared Home without authority leakage;
5. export readable records and recover the scoped state.

The public line is:

> **Steward the property without owning the resident's life.**
