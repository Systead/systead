# Brand asset usage

**Status:** Working brand standard
**Brand:** Systead

## Asset roles

### Flagship header

Files:

- `assets/img/brand/systead-header.png`
- `assets/img/brand/systead-header.webp`

Use for:

- repository introduction;
- product landing-page masthead;
- major presentation title;
- announcement art;
- high-level brand context.

Do not use as a tiny navigation logo. The wordmark and tagline require space.

### Compact logo

Files:

- `assets/img/brand/systead-compact-logo.png`
- `assets/img/brand/systead-compact-logo.webp`

Use for:

- organization profile;
- square social image;
- app/about surfaces;
- documentation covers;
- secondary brand presentation.

### Emblem and icons

Files:

- `assets/img/brand/systead-emblem.png`
- `assets/img/brand/systead-emblem.webp`
- `assets/img/brand/systead-icon-*.png`

Use for:

- navigation;
- favicon;
- app icon studies;
- compact badges;
- repository identity where the full wordmark is unreadable.

## Meaning

The emblem combines:

- House/home;
- stable structure;
- connected nodes;
- local intelligence;
- stewardship and controlled coordination.

## Tagline

Current primary tagline:

> **Local intelligence. Built for home.**

“Home” means the operator's owned ground: personal, household, professional, creative, and organisational work under one controlled House.

## Visual relationship to the product site

The dark metallic flagship masthead may lead into warmer, quieter product pages.

This contrast is intentional:

- dark masthead → identity, permanence, trust, and gravity;
- warm product surface → usability, calm, humanity, and daily work;
- dark technical sections → machinery, architecture, and control.

## Avoid

- stretching or distorting the mark;
- placing the flagship header in cramped cards;
- using the full header as a favicon;
- adding unrelated glow-heavy SaaS graphics;
- merging the mark with Stokknes Publishing heraldry or literary imagery;
- changing the tagline without a recorded decision;
- treating generated raster artwork as a final trademark-production master.

## Production note

The current assets are working raster brand assets. Before formal commercial release, create controlled vector masters, spacing rules, monochrome variants, accessibility checks, and trademark-ready source files.
